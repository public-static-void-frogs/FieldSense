# FrogFarmers team portraits

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Each portrait is a separate generated raster illustration, delivered to the app as a 768 × 512 WebP. The original generated PNGs remain in the generator output directory.

## Generation prompts and final assets

### Oleh

Final asset: `public/images/team/oleh.webp`

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: individual team profile card illustration for FrogFarmers, a farming data website. Create ONE cute anthropomorphic green frog, charming high-end storybook editorial illustration with soft clay-like shapes, painted gouache texture, large expressive round eyes, gentle cream highlights, subtle paper grain, rich forest greens. Landscape 3:2 composition, centered waist-up character, face fully visible, small farming context and simple color backdrop. All six team portraits must feel like the same collectible illustrated character family. No text, letters, logos, watermarks, photorealism, humans, or extra frogs. Oleh: confident friendly leader frog farmer in rolled-up work shirt and dark green overalls with a straw hat, using a laptop and holding a small farm planning clipboard. Frontend and backend builder, warm capable expression. Pale sunny yellow backdrop, tiny wheat sprouts and blue-green laptop. Medium-wide waist-up portrait, all face, hat and hands inside frame.
